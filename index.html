What we do on every project

Six things a company cannot get from any single institute cell.

01

Cross-institution problem breakdown

One problem often needs materials, design and electronics together. We split it and place each part with the right professor, across different institutions if needed.

02

Project scoping

A relationship manager turns a vague brief into deliverables, a budget and a schedule that both sides sign before work starts.

03

Milestone-linked payments

Payment releases against agreed milestones, not against time spent. The company can exit early. The professor gets paid on progress.

04

Professor scorecard

On-time delivery, client rating, output quality and repeat-hire rate. A record that follows the professor and drives future matching.

05

IP and patent filing

We manage drafting and filing, using the expedited route open to educational institutions. Ownership is agreed in writing before day one.

06

Legal and money handling

NDAs, service agreements, institute overhead compliance and payment routing. The company signs once and deals with one party.

What we guarantee

We guarantee the process. Not the science.

The honest version of what any research-driven partner should tell you.

We guarantee process

Every project runs on the same rails.

  • Reply within 24 hours
  • Written progress report every two weeks
  • A named person, not a helpdesk
  • Escalation within 72 hours of a missed milestone
  • Fail on any of these and our fee for that period is refunded
We guarantee people

If a professor withdraws, we replace them.

  • If a professor goes silent, slips past the agreed threshold or withdraws, we replace them at our cost
  • The company never pays twice for the same milestone
  • Scorecard history follows the researcher and drives future matches
  • Institute-level continuity where feasible for a project reset
We do not guarantee

Outcome certainty in science does not exist.

  • No promise of a specific research finding or a patent grant
  • Liability capped at fees paid to us
  • Delays caused by the client stop the clock
  • A written change-order process for scope shifts

The vision

India should be able to turn academic capability into real-world industrial outcomes at scale. Not as a series of ad-hoc referrals from one institute cell to one company, but as an operating layer that anyone can reach.

Trust · Confidentiality · IP

Because most industrial problems are sensitive.

Northbridge handles technical problems that a company would not put on a public LinkedIn post. So privacy has to be part of the product — not a paragraph in the footer.

01
Three levels of visibility

Public sector information stays open. Project details become visible only to verified parties. NDA-protected information is confidential and encrypted end to end. You choose the level for every project.

02
NDA before any detail moves

For encrypted matching, mutual NDA is signed before we speak to any professor about your work. No leakage in the discovery process.

03
IP is agreed in writing before day one

We use standard IP frameworks aligned with institute rules. Ownership, background IP, foreground IP and licensing are documented before the professor sees the brief.

04
Verified profiles, not scraped ones

Every professor on Northbridge is verified through their institutional email and profile. We never claim a professor is on the network without their consent.

05
Milestone-linked money, held safely

Payments release against agreed milestones. Funds are held in a client account, not commingled with operating money. Full audit trail for the company and the institution.
